# Grand Horizon Hotel & Resort — Booking Policies

**Document Type:** Knowledge Base Article
**Category:** Reservations & Guest Policies
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-01
**Applicable Properties:** All Grand Horizon Hotel & Resort locations

## 1. Reservation Policy

### 1.1 How to Book
Reservations can be made through:
- The official website (grandhorizonhotels.com)
- The Grand Horizon mobile app
- Phone reservations (+1-800-555-0199)
- Authorized third-party travel agents (Expedia, Booking.com, etc.)

### 1.2 Booking Confirmation
- A confirmation email with a unique Booking Reference Number (format: GH-XXXXXXX) is sent within 15 minutes of booking.
- Guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID and the credit card used for booking at check-in.
- Group bookings (10+ rooms) require a signed contract and are handled by the Groups & Events desk.

### 1.3 Rate Types
| Rate Type | Payment Timing | Flexibility |
|---|---|---|
| Flexible Rate | Charged at check-in | Free cancellation up to 24 hrs before arrival |
| Advance Purchase | Charged in full at booking | Non-refundable, non-changeable |
| Member Rate | Charged at check-in | Free cancellation up to 48 hrs before arrival (loyalty members only) |
| Corporate Rate | Charged at check-in | Free cancellation up to 24 hrs before arrival (valid corporate ID required) |

## 2. Check-in / Check-out Policy

- **Standard Check-in:** 3:00 PM local time
- **Standard Check-out:** 11:00 AM local time
- **Early Check-in:** Subject to availability; may incur a fee of 50% of one night's rate if before 10:00 AM
- **Late Check-out:** Until 2:00 PM is complimentary for loyalty members (Gold tier and above); otherwise a fee of 50% of one night's rate applies
- **Minimum Check-in Age:** 18 years, unless accompanied by a parent/guardian
- Guests arriving after 11:00 PM must notify the hotel in advance via the "Late Arrival" option during booking to guarantee the room.

---

## 3. Cancellation & No-Show Policy

### 3.1 Cancellation Windows
- **Flexible Rate:** Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the check-in date (local hotel time). Cancellations made after this window incur a charge equal to one night's stay plus applicable taxes.
- **Advance Purchase Rate:** Non-refundable at time of booking. No cancellations or date changes permitted.
- **Peak Season / Holiday Bookings (Dec 20–Jan 5, major local holidays):** Require 7 days' notice for free cancellation regardless of rate type.

### 3.2 No-Show Policy
- If a guest does not arrive and does not cancel by the deadline, the full first night's charge (plus tax) is billed to the card on file.
- The remainder of the reservation (subsequent nights) will be automatically released unless the guest contacts the hotel.

### 3.3 Refund Processing
- Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method.
- Refunds for bookings made through third-party platforms must be requested through that platform.

---

## 4. Modification Policy

- Date, room type, or occupancy changes are permitted **only** for Flexible, Member, and Corporate rates, subject to availability.
- Modifications must be made at least 24 hours before check-in.
- Advance Purchase bookings cannot be modified; guests must cancel (forfeiting payment) and rebook.
- Any modification that results in a rate change will be charged or refunded the difference.

---

## 5. Payment & Deposit Policy

- Accepted payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and UnionPay.
- A valid credit card is required to guarantee all bookings; debit cards may be used but are subject to a pre-authorization hold.
- Pre-authorization holds:
  - Standard rooms: hold equal to one night's rate + estimated incidentals ($50/night)
  - Suites: hold equal to one night's rate + estimated incidentals ($150/night)
- Currency is charged in the local currency of the property unless otherwise stated.
- Resort fees (where applicable) are mandatory and disclosed at the time of booking; they are **not** included in the room rate.

---

## 6. Occupancy & Extra Guest Policy

- Standard room occupancy: 2 adults. Maximum occupancy varies by room type (see room descriptions).
- Children under 12 stay free when using existing bedding.
- Extra adult charge: $35/night (subject to property maximum occupancy limits).
- Rollaway beds and cribs are available on request, subject to availability and a $20/night fee for rollaways (cribs are complimentary).
- Pets: Only permitted at designated "Pet-Friendly" properties; a non-refundable pet fee of $75/stay applies, maximum 2 pets per room, weight limit 50 lbs per pet.

## 8. Special Circumstances

### 8.1 Force Majeure
In cases of natural disasters, government travel restrictions, or other events beyond guest control, cancellation fees may be waived at management's discretion upon review of supporting documentation.

### 8.2 Medical Emergencies
Guests unable to travel due to a documented medical emergency may request a fee waiver by submitting medical documentation to guestrelations@grandhorizonhotels.com within 30 days of the original check-in date.

### 8.3 Overbooking / Walked Guests
In the rare event a confirmed reservation cannot be honored due to overbooking, the hotel will:
1. Arrange comparable or superior accommodation at a nearby property at no additional cost.
2. Cover reasonable transportation to the alternate property.
3. Provide a compensation credit of $100 toward a future stay.

**Q: How do I get a refund after cancelling?**

**Q: Is there a minimum age to check in?**
A: Yes, guests must be at least 18 years old unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.

**Q: What happens if the hotel is overbooked and I have a confirmed reservation?**
A: You will be relocated to a comparable or better nearby hotel at no extra cost, with transportation covered and a $100 credit toward a future stay.
